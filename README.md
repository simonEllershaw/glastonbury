## 🛒 Booking

It's simple (I promise...)
1. Get as many "groups of 6" on the spreadsheet
1. Everyone tries to get through 6pm 1st Oct (coach) AND 9am 4th October (non-coach)
1. You get through (WOOO!)
1. Select a group in priority order of:
    - Your group (if does not have tickets)
    - The next group in the spreadsheet that does not have tickets
    - On 1st October obviously you can only select a coach group
1. Announce on whatsapp group "BUYING FOR [GROUP NAME]"
1. Buy the tickets
    - Copy and paste info from spreadsheet
    - Deposit is £75 per person + coach fare (£450+)
        - Ensure you can pay the deposit for the whole group before trying to get through (obviously no worries if not it's a lot of ££££)
        - The group must pay back the AMAZING PERSON that got them tickets THE SAME DAY
1. If payment fails
    - Announce on whatsapp group "RELEASE FOR [GROUP NAME]"
1. If payment suceeds
    - Update the spreadsheet with BOUGHT for the relevant group
1. We keep trying till every group has a ticket or they are sold out
